How to play

🧩 Above every column and at the start of every row there is a clue: a picture or a word (a synonym). The small number is the word's length. Tap a clue to highlight its word.

🔤 Each turn you get a set of letters. Drag a letter and drop it on the right square. (You can also tap a letter, then tap a square.)

✅ Correct letter = 1 point. When a word is completed, whoever placed most of its letters gets bonus points equal to its length.

❌ A wrong letter costs 1 point and ends your turn.

⏭️ Pass skips your turn and gives you new letters. After you place at least one letter, the button becomes End turn.

🤖 Then the computer plays. Score more than the computer to win and unlock the next level.

🆘 Helpers:
💡 Reveal: pick a square to show its letter.
🎯 Hint: lights up one of your letters and the square it belongs to.

📈 As you progress the grid grows, the words get longer and harder, more clues become words instead of pictures, and the computer gets smarter.

⭐ Stars: a win = 1 star, +1 star for winning by 5 or more, +1 star for using no helpers.
